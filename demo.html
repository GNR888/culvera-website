THE TECHNOLOGY

Inside the prototype

The current Culvera prototype connects multiple public agricultural and environmental data sources into a single analysis pipeline.

NASA POWER

Daily weather and climate variables.

SOILGRIDS

Location specific soil characteristics including pH, nitrogen, organic carbon and texture.

SENTINEL2

Satellite imagery used to derive vegetation health signals such as NDVI.

MACHINE LEARNING

Prototype classification and regression models turn these inputs into crop suitability and yield estimates.

AGRONOMIC LOGIC

Soil, climate and seasonal conditions are translated into practical planning information.
